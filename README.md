# 📊 Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built using Power BI to evaluate macroeconomic store health, track retail profit optimizations, analyze regional segment contributions, and pinpoint the direct impact of discounting strategies on net profit margins.

---

### 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Captured
* **Total Sales Revenue:** $187K
* **Total Profit:** $6.69K
* **Total Units Sold:** 1,632 units
* **Average Discount Rate:** 14% (0.14)

---

### 💡 Data Insights & Business Metrics
* **Monthly Sales Volatility:** Sales experienced strong seasonal growth, spiking dramatically in **July ($18.7K)** and **February ($15.8K)**, while hitting annual floors in October ($9.3K).
* **Discount Impact on Profitability:** A deep dive into category variables reveals that high discount rates significantly impact sub-categories like **Binders**, **Storage**, and **Copiers**, drawing clear correlations between aggressive pricing strategies and compressed margins.
* **Product Category Breakdown:** 
  * **Technology:** Drives the highest category revenue share at **$21.82K** (led by Phones) and **$12.70K** (led by Copiers).
  * **Furniture:** Maintained robust sales metrics led by Tables ($19.48K) and Chairs ($17.66K).
  * **Office Supplies:** Anchored by Binders ($16.99K) and Storage ($19.26K).
* **Regional Segment Performance:** The **West** region consistently generated top-tier sales volume across all major buyer cohorts (**Consumer ($18.8K)**, **Corporate ($13.1K)**, and **Home Office ($12.5K)**), outpacing the Central, East, and South territories.
* **Profit Margin Bottlenecks:** A waterfall analysis shows that while most sub-categories steadily increased net margins, **Tables** caused a noticeable profit drop (-$0.1K), identifying a critical area for pricing structure adjustments.

---

### 🛠️ Power BI Features & DAX Implementation
* **Dynamic Time Series:** Area and line charts tracking monthly sales growth and fluctuations across the fiscal calendar.
* **Advanced Analytical Layouts:** Integrated scatter charts to map *Discount vs. Profit* by Sub-Category alongside a margin Waterfall chart to track net profit drivers.
* **Cross-Filtering & Drill-Downs:** Full interactive workspace mapping data relationships across *Customer Segments*, *Regions*, and *Sub-Categories*.
* **Date Slicers:** Implemented an dynamic interactive timeline filter spanning from early 2023 to early 2024.
